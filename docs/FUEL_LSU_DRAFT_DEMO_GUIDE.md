# Demonstrate the FUEL/LSU feedstock drafts

Use the existing FUEL/LSU demo login sheet at [the development login page](https://eco-globe-dev-web.vercel.app/login). The account passwords and permissions have not changed.

## Show buyer requirements

1. Sign in to the company you want to demonstrate.
2. Open **Marketplace → Requests for quote**.
3. Select **Draft**.
4. Expand a material to show its source notes, supplied quantity and missing details.
5. For an account with write access, select **Resume draft** to open the saved requirement.
6. Select **Save draft** to keep the requirement private.

Zentech has the supplied **20 metric tonnes of sugarcane bagasse ash**. Other quantities and all prices remain unset. NovaSpark has one **Fuel requirement (unconfirmed)** draft and retains read-only access. Its possible fuels are notes, not confirmed demand.

## Show seller products

For BB&M Materials, Encore CO2, Cavendish Energy, Renovigo Chemicals or Waste to Energy Systems:

1. Open the account menu.
2. Select **Switch to Seller**.
3. Open **Listings**.
4. Open a **Draft** product to show its source description and missing supply details.
5. Select **Edit listing** to review the saved fields.
6. Select **Save changes** to retain the draft.

An incomplete draft can be saved without a facility. Submission still requires the missing supply details and a genuine seller SDS. Do not submit presentation drafts before Ana and Bea confirm those details.

## Check visibility

1. Compare the company’s draft list with the [verified inventory](verification/2026-10-16-fuel-lsu-feedstock-drafts.md).
2. Open the buyer **Open** filter. The new presentation drafts must not appear there.
3. Check public browsing. The presentation drafts must not appear there either.
4. Sign in to another company. It must not have access to the first company’s private drafts.

ApplicaRAD, CodeGig, Westfield and Eray have no feedstock drafts. Their technology or equipment services have not been turned into material listings.
